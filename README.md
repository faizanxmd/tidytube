# Tidy Tube

A lightweight Chrome extension to clean up YouTube and browse Hacker News video picks.

## Features

- **Hide clutter** — One-click toggles in the popup for Shorts, Music, Mixes, Politics & news, TV & late night, plus your own custom channel/keyword filters.
- **Clean watch history** — A one-time, preview-first tool on YouTube's History page to remove unwanted videos (Hacker News picks are protected).
- **Hacker News picks** — Adds a Hacker News entry to YouTube's left sidebar to browse curated talks, lectures, and explainers from [HackerTube](https://hackertube.org).

## Install

1. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder.
