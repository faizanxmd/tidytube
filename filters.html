Filters

Each category is a list of channels and title words. Turn on what it should do:

History
Clean up history on YouTube's History page removes it, so YouTube stops recommending from it.
Hide
Kept out of Home, search and the sidebar beside videos.
Tell
Tidy Tube tells YouTube “Not interested”, a few videos per page.

How matching works

Channels: one per line, the channel's name or its @handle. Case, spaces and punctuation don't matter.

Title words: one per line, a word or phrase that must appear whole in the title. * stands for any letters, so election* also finds “elections”. For anything fancier, write a regular expression between slashes: /senate (race|map)/.
